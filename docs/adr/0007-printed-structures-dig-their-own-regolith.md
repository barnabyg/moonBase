# Printed Structures are paid for in excavator time, not stockpiled Regolith

The regolith printer builds Regolith shielding, Sintered Regolith shielding and the sintered landing pad from Regolith the excavator digs on the site during a longer Site Preparation: 6 hours of digging on top of the grading. Nothing comes out of the base's Regolith stock, and Assembly never waits for it. The cost of the material is the excavator's time and Dust wear, and the work it can't do meanwhile: other Construction's Site Preparation, and digging for the Plants.

We considered keeping the Regolith free ("straight from the ground") and only saying so, but then the excavator and digging have no bearing on printed Construction, and a printed pad is a free alternative to a $30M Launch purchase. Drawing the Regolith from the stockpile in batches would need a stock and a digging rate many times larger: a pad's slab is 40 to 70 t, against a 2000 kg stock filled at 50 kg an hour, and printed builds would starve the Plants. Keeping the excavator at the site through Assembly, alongside the printer, would need two Machines on one stage, which nothing else does. The 6 hours are a playable abstraction, not the time it would take to dig that much Regolith at the stockpile's rate.
