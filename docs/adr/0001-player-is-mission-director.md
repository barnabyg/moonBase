# The player is a Mission Director, not an avatar

The player directs the base from above, placing plans and setting priorities. Robots, and later Crew, claim the resulting Work Orders and carry them out on their own, and the player never micromanages individual Robots. We rejected a Satisfactory-style embodied avatar because the game's premise is that nothing appears by magic. That premise comes across most clearly when the player is _not_ the builder and has to watch real machines do the work slowly, sometimes unsuccessfully. An avatar would also have pulled most of the effort into character controllers and first-person art.

The Mission Director is still a real, mortal person at the base, never shown on screen, who lives in the spacecraft at first and then in a Habitat. Their survival is the player's most direct personal stake, so their death ends the Campaign. In the first version they are the only human. Crew are deferred.
