# Late-game Science comes from what the player builds and supplies

By Act 4 the base's Science comes almost all from the Research queue, which runs one Research Task at a time and needs nothing to run. Act 4's tasks run for up to 555 Earth Days and pay about 0.14 Science an hour, so the player can do nothing to bring the next Design closer and is left waiting. Surveys pay too little for the time they take to change that.

We will add three late Structures that earn Science, or speed it up, as long as the player builds them out and keeps them supplied. Each follows the Greenhouse's rules: its work comes in rounds (or, for the lab, in game-hours of speed-up), it takes what it uses from the base's stocks a share at a time and never below the reserve, it draws power as a Structure and a Night load do, and a shortage loses only the round it is on.

- **An infrared telescope.** It observes only at Night, on a round of the observing programme's kind but paying several times more. It can't observe while a lit Structure is within a set distance of it, so it is built away from the base and needs a long cable. Its near-infrared camera is cooled by boiling off propellant, so its Science competes with the propellant the base sells.
- **A radio telescope array.** Antenna fields the Robot lays out one at a time, each adding to the array's rate. It works through Daylight and Night, but can't work while a Plant is within a set distance of it, so it too is built away from the base. It needs many Components and a lot of cable more than power.
- **An automated experiments lab.** While it has its power, water and oxygen, the Research Task running advances faster, by a set amount for each lab. It changes no Research Task, so the queue stays the one place Research Tasks are run, and its 3-task limit still brings the player back.

The Research Tasks and the Designs' prices stay as they are, so a base that builds none of these takes as long as it does today. What changes is how long Act 4 takes for a player who does build them, and that is the point: Act 4's length becomes the player's to shorten. Surveys also find the dark and radio-quiet sites the two telescopes need, so Expeditions have a part in Act 4.

These are new Structures. The telescope the base already has is unchanged, as an upgrade never changes what exists.

## Considered options

- **Make the late Research Tasks shorter, or pay more.** This makes the wait shorter, but leaves nothing for the player to do in it.
- **Pay Surveys more.** This gives the Robot something to do, but it is still a wait, and Expeditions already have a role in the resource economy that higher Science payments would distort.
- **More slots in the Research queue, run in parallel.** This doubles the rate without the player building or supplying anything, and changes the queue's rules more than a speed-up does.
- **Reprice Act 4's Research Tasks down, so the long-game play guide still ends on Earth Day 3,000 only with the new Structures.** This keeps ADR-0008's 10 hours but makes a base without the new Structures wait even longer than it does now. We chose to leave a passive base's length alone and let an active one finish sooner. ADR-0008's 10-hour figure becomes what the Research queue alone takes, not what every player sees.
