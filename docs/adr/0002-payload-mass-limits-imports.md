# Imports are limited by Payload Mass per Launch, as well as by Funding

Everything brought from Earth arrives on a Launch with a fixed Payload Mass, at a limited cadence and after a transit time, and each Launch also costs Funding. We rejected a money-only economy because cheap shipping would make local manufacturing pointless. Mass is what makes "ship it or build it here?" a real decision, and that decision is the core of the build-order gameplay.
